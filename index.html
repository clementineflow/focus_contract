Focus Contract Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 25, 2026

Focus Contract values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use Focus Contract (the “App”).

1. Information We Collect

Focus Contract is designed to minimize data collection.

We may process the following types of information:

App Usage and Focus Data

To provide features such as focus sessions, Pauses, Focus Trigger, and session history, the App may store information on your device, including:

  • Focus session duration
  • Whether a session was completed or ended early
  • Pause usage
  • Focus Trigger settings
  • Local App settings and preferences

This information is primarily stored on your device.

Screen Time and Family Controls Data

Focus Contract uses Apple’s Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity APIs to provide features such as app blocking and Focus Trigger.

The App does not access the content of other apps, including:

  • Messages
  • Photos
  • Passwords
  • Browsing content
  • Keyboard input

Apple’s Screen Time APIs may provide the App with limited information necessary to apply restrictions or determine whether usage thresholds have been reached.

We do not sell this information or use it for advertising purposes.

2. Accounts and Backend Services

Focus Contract may use Supabase to provide necessary backend services.

These services may be used to:

  • Create an anonymous user identifier
  • Store Pause balances
  • Prevent duplicate rewards
  • Verify in-app purchases
  • Verify Early Exit payments
  • Prevent duplicate transaction processing

Unless we introduce an account feature that explicitly asks you to provide personal information in the future, Focus Contract does not require your name, address, or other directly identifying personal information.

3. In-App Purchases and Payments

Focus Contract uses Apple StoreKit and App Store In-App Purchase to process payments.

Payment information, such as your credit card number, is not provided directly to Focus Contract and is not stored by us.

To verify purchases and prevent duplicate transaction processing, we may process transaction identifiers and transaction verification information provided by Apple.

Payments are processed by Apple and are subject to Apple’s applicable privacy policies and terms.

4. Error and Diagnostic Data

To improve App stability and fix technical issues, Focus Contract may use error-monitoring services such as Sentry.

When an error or crash occurs, limited technical information may be collected, including:

  • App version
  • iOS version
  • Device type
  • Error messages
  • Crash information
  • Technical diagnostic information related to the issue

This information is used only to identify, analyze, and resolve technical problems. It is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

5. We Do Not Sell Your Personal Information

Focus Contract does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We also do not use Screen Time or focus-related information to create advertising profiles.

6. Third-Party Services

Focus Contract may use the following third-party services to provide certain features:

  • Apple — App Store, StoreKit, Family Controls, and Device Activity
  • Supabase — Backend database, anonymous identity, and transaction-related services
  • Sentry — Error and crash diagnostics

These third-party services may process necessary information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide App functionality, process transactions, prevent abuse, troubleshoot issues, or comply with legal obligations.

Information stored locally on your device may be removed when you delete the App.

Certain payment, transaction verification, or backend balance records may need to be retained for a longer period to prevent duplicate transaction processing, handle refunds, or resolve payment-related issues.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through Focus Contract.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children’s Privacy

Focus Contract is not specifically designed for children.

We do not knowingly request or collect children’s names, addresses, or other directly identifying personal information.

If you believe that we have unintentionally collected personal information that should not have been collected, please contact us.

10. Your Rights

Depending on the laws that apply in your jurisdiction, you may have the right to request:

  • Access to personal information we hold about you
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion of certain information
  • Restriction of or objection to certain processing activities

To make such a request, you may contact us through the contact options provided within the Focus Contract App.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our features, services, or legal requirements.

If we make material changes, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, your data, or Focus Contract, please contact us through the contact options provided within the Focus Contract App.

By using Focus Contract, you acknowledge the information practices described in this Privacy Policy.
